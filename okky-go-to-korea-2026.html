Why Korea is different

Market entry is not a translation project.

The hard part is building trust, finding the right people and learning how decisions are actually made.

01

The first hire matters disproportionately.

A strong first local leader or technical hire can compress years of trial and error. The wrong one can isolate headquarters from the market.

02

Developer trust is earned inside the ecosystem.

For technical products, awareness is not enough. Engineers need credible local voices, useful content, peers and real product experience.

03

Relationships unlock customers and partners.

Enterprise buyers, implementation partners and technical leaders often move through trusted networks long before a formal procurement process begins.
